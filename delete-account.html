Delete My Account

Want to leave? We'll make it easy. Your data, your choice.

⚠️

Account deletion is permanent

Once your account is deleted, all your personal data, order history, and saved addresses will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days. This action cannot be undone. If you have any pending orders or active deliveries, please wait for them to complete before requesting deletion.

🗑️

Permanent Deletion

Your account and all personal data will be permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers within 30 days of your request.

⏱️

30-Day Processing

We process all deletion requests within 30 days as required by the Indian IT Act, 2000. You will receive an email confirmation once done.

📧

Email Confirmation

After submitting your request, you'll receive a confirmation email within 2 business days acknowledging receipt of your request.

🔒

Secure Process

Your request will be verified against your registered email address to prevent unauthorized deletions. Your security is our priority.

How to Request Account Deletion

1

Send an email from your registered address

Use the same email address you used to sign up for Streat Eats. Requests from unregistered emails cannot be processed for security reasons.

2

Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"

This helps us identify and prioritize your request in our support queue. You can use the email template below.

3

Include your registered phone number

To verify your identity, mention the phone number linked to your Streat Eats account in the email body.

4

Wait for confirmation — up to 2 business days

We'll send a confirmation email acknowledging your request. Actual data deletion happens within 30 days from the confirmation date.
