Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's how we handle your data.

Last Updated: May 2026  |  Governing Law: Indian IT Act, 2000  |  Contact: privacy@theStreateats.in

Welcome to Streat Eats ("we", "our", "us"). We are committed to protecting the personal information of our users, vendors, and delivery partners. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights as a user. By using the Streat Eats app or website, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you register or use the Streat Eats app, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address(es)
  • Profile photo (optional)

1.2 Order and Transaction Data

We collect data related to your orders and transactions, including:

  • Order history (items ordered, amounts, timestamps)
  • Payment method used (COD or online)
  • Delivery location details
  • Ratings and reviews you submit

1.3 Location Data

We collect your device location when you use the app in order to find nearby vendors and assign delivery riders accurately. Location access is only active while the app is in use and only with your permission. You may disable location access through your device settings, but this will limit delivery functionality.

1.4 Device and Technical Data

We automatically collect certain technical information including device type, operating system version, app version, IP address, and general usage analytics. This data is used solely for performance monitoring and bug fixing.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your food orders
  • Coordinating delivery through our rider network
  • Sending order confirmation, delivery updates, and support notifications
  • Improving app features and user experience based on usage patterns
  • Sending promotional communications (only with your consent — unsubscribe anytime)
  • Preventing fraud, fake orders, and platform misuse
  • Complying with applicable Indian laws and regulations

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties for commercial use. Your data may be shared only in these limited circumstances:

  • Delivery Riders: Your name, address, and order details are shared only with the assigned rider for delivery completion.
  • Vendors: The vendor fulfilling your order receives only the items ordered and special instructions — not your personal contact details.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if required by Indian law, court order, or government authority.
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger or acquisition, user data may transfer to the acquiring entity and you will be notified in advance.

4. Third-Party Services

Streat Eats uses the following trusted third-party services to operate the platform. Each has their own privacy policies governing use of your data:

  • Supabase — Backend database and user authentication
  • Firebase (Google) — Push notifications and crash analytics
  • Cashfree Payments — Online payment processing

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), secure backend storage via Supabase, and strict access control. In the unlikely event of a data breach, we will notify affected users within 72 hours as required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. If you delete your account, your personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days, except for data required to comply with legal obligations such as financial transaction records.

7. Your Rights

As a Streat Eats user, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Correction: Update or correct inaccurate personal information
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated personal data
  • Right to Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Opt out of promotional communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@theStreateats.in with your registered email address. We will respond within 7 business days.

8. Cookie Policy

The Streat Eats website uses minimal cookies to ensure proper site functioning. We use essential cookies for session management, and anonymous analytics cookies to improve the website. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

9. Children's Privacy

Streat Eats is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete the data promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you via app notification or email at least 7 days before changes take effect. Continued use of Streat Eats after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, applicable in India. Any disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of courts in Haldwani, Uttarakhand.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@theStreateats.in
  • General Support: support@theStreateats.in
