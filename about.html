About Streat Eats

The story behind Haldwani's first street food delivery platform

📖 Our story

Born in Haldwani,
Built for Haldwani

Streat Eats was founded in 2026 by four friends from Haldwani who loved street food but wanted a smarter way to enjoy it at home. Late evenings, the craving for hot momos or crispy chowmein would hit — but stepping out wasn't always possible.

We noticed that Zomato and Swiggy only served restaurants. The beloved local momos stall, the chaat corner, the burger thela — all completely ignored by the big platforms. These vendors had loyal customers but zero delivery reach.

So we built Streat Eats — Haldwani's own hyperlocal street food delivery platform, made by locals, for locals. Every order you place directly supports a small local vendor and a local delivery partner.
